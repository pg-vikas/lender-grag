Create a large scale premium background animation for Lender Greg’s website showing a house being built piece by piece.

This is a visual storytelling element, not a cartoon. It should feel elegant, modern, architectural, and high end. The animation should represent the idea of building a home, building trust, and building a future. It needs to be beautiful, subtle enough to live in the background, but still clearly visible and impressive.

The house should gradually assemble itself in a smooth, sophisticated sequence. Think of a premium agency or luxury fintech website background animation, not a children’s construction animation and not a cheap explainer graphic.

CORE IDEA

A house forms piece by piece in the background through a layered animated build sequence.

The assembly should feel intentional and graceful:
- foundation appears
- floor plane forms
- framing rises
- walls or wall outlines assemble
- roof structure forms
- windows and doors appear
- subtle finishing details lock in
- the full home settles into a polished final state

This should loop elegantly or remain in a final finished state with subtle ambient motion after the full build completes.

ANIMATION SEQUENCE

Create an animation sequence where the house builds in stages.

Suggested sequence:
1. Ground plane or foundation line fades in
2. Base slab or floor outline draws itself
3. Structural framing rises upward in clean lines
4. Exterior wall planes or outlines animate into place
5. Roof geometry assembles
6. Window and door openings appear
7. Small polished details fade in
8. Finished house settles into a calm, elegant final state
9. Subtle ambient motion continues after completion such as glow drift, slight line shimmer, floating particles, or soft depth movement

The motion should feel smooth, premium, and satisfying.
