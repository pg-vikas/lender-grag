CALCULATION LOGIC

Implement realistic mortgage style calculations.

Mortgage Calculator logic should include:
- Loan amount = home price minus down payment
- Monthly principal and interest based on amortization formula
- Monthly property tax estimate
- Monthly home insurance estimate
- HOA monthly fee
- PMI or mortgage insurance when down payment is below threshold if enabled

Affordability Calculator logic should include:
- Determine monthly income from annual income
- Use debt to income assumptions
- Conservative mode should use more cautious monthly budget assumptions
- Aggressive mode should use higher budget tolerance
- Subtract monthly debts
- Estimate affordable monthly housing payment
- Reverse calculate approximate affordable home price based on rate, term, taxes, insurance, and PMI assumptions

Include sensible default values and make assumptions editable where useful.
